Password Reset Guide

If you forgot your password, you can recover your account using the password reset process.

Steps:
1. Open the login page.
2. Click "Forgot Password".
3. Enter the email address associated with your account.
4. Check your email for the password recovery link.
5. Open the link and create a new password.

The password reset link is valid for 30 minutes.

If you do not receive the email, check your spam folder.
